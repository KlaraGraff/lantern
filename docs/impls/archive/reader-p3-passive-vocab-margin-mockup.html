Chapter Eight

Another Way into Orgoreyn

It is a durable, mysterious kind of loyalty. It has survived cold, distance, and everything neither of us has known how to say.

The long crossing had made us economical with words, but not with attention. Across the ice the light was changing, slowly and without warmth.

The horizon appeared close enough to touch and far enough to contain another country. He turned toward me, waiting for a question I had not yet learned to ask.

Ambiguous promises can be comforting, but they rarely survive the journey into daylight. The answer remained provisional.
