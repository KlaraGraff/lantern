Chapter Four

The Power of Three

Language is built from patterns, and the most satisfying pattern is often the simplest. Three examples feel complete without becoming predictable.

Writers have understood this instinctively for centuries. A balanced sentence makes a difficult idea easier to follow, because rhythm quietly tells the reader where one thought finishes and another begins.

好的排版不是装饰，而是一种安静的引导。 It does not ask to be noticed, yet it lets reading continue naturally.

The voice continues to the next paragraph and turns the page only when the active sentence moves beyond the visible reading area.

Repetition can give a sentence weight, but variation gives it life.

The reader hears the pattern before consciously recognising it, and the thought arrives with the rhythm already attached.

这也是为什么朗读不应该在页面边界突然停下。 A book is continuous even when its pages are not.
