¶1For decades, the Riverside district was known mainly for department stores and parking structures. When a luxury mall was proposed on the site of a nineteenth-century market, officials initially treated the buried remains as a technical obstacle. Yet local historian Mara Chen argued that the city's planning philosophy deserved scrutiny. She believed that choosing to build first and ask historical questions later was not a neutral decision.

rig
动词 · 原文形态 rigged · CET-6
语境释义
（临时地）装配、搭设。文中指工人在井口临时架起一套滑轮装置——强调因地制宜、手工拼装，而不是安装标准设备。
例句
They rigged a shelter from branches and a tarp.
他们用树枝和一块防水布搭了个临时遮蔽处。
